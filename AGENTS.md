# Workspace instructions

Before writing or editing any CV, cover letter, statement or application response in this folder, read `WRITING_STYLE_GUIDE.md` and follow it.

Keep all claims traceable to the user's own record: `EVIDENCE_BANK.md`, the master CV in `master/`, their papers and repositories, or information they supplied. Never invent experience, metrics, tools, collaborations or outcomes to match a job description.

New applications start from the master CV in `master/` and `PROFILE.md`. Keep tracker IDs permanent; do not calculate them from row positions. Before a submission, the user keeps the exact files that were uploaded.
