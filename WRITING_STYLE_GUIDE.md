# Writing style guide (starter; edit to match your voice)

## Rules for CVs and cover letters

- Plain, specific sentences. Say what you did, with what, and the measured result if there is one.
- Every claim traces to `EVIDENCE_BANK.md` or the master CV. If it cannot be traced, leave it out.
- State gaps plainly and briefly in the cover letter, once, then say what you would bring.
- Use one consistent form for dates, degree status and job titles in every document.
- ASCII hyphens only; no en or em dashes (they are a common marker of machine-written text).

## Phrases to avoid

passionate, excited to, dynamic, results-driven, proven track record, team player, fast learner, synergy, leverage (as a verb), cutting-edge, world-class, thrilled, I believe I would be a great fit

## Cover-letter test

1. Why this job and this employer, in one or two sentences that could not go to ten other employers.
2. Two or three pieces of your work that directly help with the duties.
3. The remaining gap, stated plainly.
4. Why the application is still reasonable.

Keep it to one page.
